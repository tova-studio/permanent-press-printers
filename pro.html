Our speciality

What is UV offset printing, and why does it matter?

“Specialising in UV offset printing enables us to print onto a wide variety of substrates with inks that dry & adhere instantaneously.”

Ordinary inks need to soak into paper to dry, so they don't work well on smooth surfaces like plastic and vinyl. UV inks are cured by ultraviolet light as they are printed. The ink sets on the surface straight away and stays put.

That is why Permanent Press can print plant tags, ID cards, labels and packaging on materials that a standard print shop may not take on.

  1. Which materials can you print on?

    Plastics, vinyl and cardboard. These are the materials our plastic, packaging, vinyl and cardboard services are built around.

  2. Inks that dry instantly

    UV inks dry and adhere instantaneously, so the print bonds to the material it's printed on.

  3. A finished look

    Work can be finished with UV gloss or matt coatings, or with spot UV matt to pick out details.
